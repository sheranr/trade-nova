# Trade Nova 2.1.0 — Luxury Edition UI/UX redesign

Design: Midnight Navy × Electric Blue × Metallic Gold (bg `#061426` / `#0B2038`, cards `#112B46`, blue `#168BFF`, gold `#F3BA55`, text `#F5F8FF` / `#A7BED4`, success `#12C996`, warning `#FFB547`, critical `#F25B65`). Inter is bundled inside the app (no internet fonts), and so is the hero artwork. The dark theme is the default, and the sun/moon button still switches to a light luxury theme.

This was a visual change only. The HS tariff data, price data, PDFs, calculations, legal text, sources and dates are unchanged.

## Regression test report (Chromium, 390 × 844, old 2.0.4 vs new 2.1.0)

- **Page text and actions:** each page's visible text and every clickable action were compared between the old and new versions.
  - **Actions:** 0 removed on all 12 pages (home, lookup, pay, desk, learn, english, team, circ, alerts, sanc, price, terms) and the Exports desk.
  - **Text removed:** none, apart from the reformatted greeting/date and the version number.
  - **Text added:** the new labels only — section titles, internet notices, the status shortcuts and the online/offline chip.
- **Functional results, identical old vs new:**
  - Home search for "palm oil" → 19 results.
  - The 4012.20 detail sheet opens.
  - Back steps work.
  - Banned list → 53 lines.
  - Duty estimator gives the same figures.
- **New features:**
  - All 7 status shortcuts filter correctly (banned 53, quarantine 12, advance pay 20, others 60 or more).
  - Recently viewed records lines, opens them and clears.
  - The online/offline chip follows the connection.
  - The splash shows about 1.1 s once per session, or about 0.2 s with reduced motion.
  - The app works from `file://` (APK mode) with 9,007 rows and Inter loaded.
- **Errors:** no JavaScript errors.
- **Visual check:** screenshots of every page in dark and light.
- **Locked data:** HS data hash checked as unchanged.
