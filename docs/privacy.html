TradeNova

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the Trade Nova Android app (Google Play and direct download) and the Trade Nova web app.

In short: Trade Nova does not ask you to sign in, does not collect or sell any personal information, and contains no ads or tracking. What you save in the app stays on your own device.

Who we are

Trade Nova is a free reference app for trade finance, imports and exports in Sri Lanka, created by Sheran Rajaguru. It is not an official app of Sri Lanka Customs, any government body or any bank.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking tools, and it does not send what you search or save to us or to anyone else.

Information stored only on your device

  • Contacts you add on the Contacts page, your watchlist, alerts and settings (for example dark or light mode).
  • Downloaded app content updates, so the app works offline.

This information never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app (or clearing its data) removes it.

Internet connections the app makes

  • Content updates. When you are online, the app downloads the latest tariff data, learning video list and notices from sheranr.github.io/trade-nova, hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address) — see the GitHub privacy statement. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • Links you open. Learning videos (Google Drive or YouTube), official sources (for example Sri Lanka Customs, CBSL, sanctions lists) and bank portals open in your browser or in the relevant app. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Reports and calls. “Report a data issue”, email, phone and WhatsApp buttons open your own email, phone or WhatsApp app. You see and choose what is sent.

Children

Trade Nova is a professional tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
